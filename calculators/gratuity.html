Gratuity Calculator

Your reward for long service.

Formula: (15/26) × last salary × years, capped at ₹20 L. Eligible after 5 years of service.

What it is

Gratuity is a lump sum an employer pays for long service, payable after five years. It is calculated as fifteen days of your last drawn salary for every completed year of service, and is tax-free up to twenty lakh.

How to use it

  1. Enter your last drawn monthly basic + DA.
  2. Set your years of service.
  3. Read the gratuity payable and the part that is tax-free.
  4. Note: you are eligible only after five years.
