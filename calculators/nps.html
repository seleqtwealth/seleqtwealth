A pension plus a lump sum.

At least 40% of the corpus must buy an annuity; the other 60% is tax-free at exit. NPS also allows an extra ₹50,000 deduction under 80CCD(1B). Illustrative only. Actual returns vary and are not guaranteed.

What it is

The National Pension System builds a retirement corpus through regular contributions until age 60. At retirement a part is taken as a lump sum and the rest buys an annuity that pays a monthly pension.

How to use it

  1. Set your monthly contribution and current age.
  2. Choose an expected return on the corpus.
  3. Set the share used to buy an annuity and its rate.
  4. Add inflation to see the pension in today's money.
