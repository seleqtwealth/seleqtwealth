What it is

A step-up SIP raises your monthly contribution by a set percentage every year, usually in step with your income. The rising amount, compounded over time, can grow your corpus far beyond a flat SIP.

How to use it

  1. Set your starting monthly amount.
  2. Choose how much to step it up each year.
  3. Set an expected return and time horizon.
  4. Compare the corpus with a flat SIP of the same start.
