What it is

Estimates the nest egg you will need to maintain your lifestyle after you stop working, allowing for inflation, and the monthly SIP that could build it in time.

How to use it

  1. Set your current and retirement age.
  2. Enter your monthly expenses today.
  3. Set inflation and your expected returns before and after retirement.
  4. Add any savings you already have to lower the SIP you need.
