Emergency Fund Calculator

A safety net of months.

Keep this money liquid (a savings account or sweep FD), not in market-linked funds. Illustrative only. Actual returns vary and are not guaranteed.

What it is

An emergency fund is cash you can reach instantly for a job loss, a medical bill or an urgent repair. The usual rule is three to six months of expenses, kept safe and liquid, not invested for growth.

How to use it

  1. Enter your monthly expenses.
  2. Choose how many months of cover you want.
  3. Add what you have set aside already.
  4. Set how much you can save each month to close the gap.
