PPF Calculator

Tax-free, government-backed.

PPF interest is fully tax-free (EEE). Rates are set by the government and revised periodically. Illustrative only. Actual returns vary and are not guaranteed.

What it is

The Public Provident Fund is a government savings scheme with a 15-year term, tax-free interest compounded yearly, and a deposit cap of ₹1.5 lakh a year. Because the interest is fully tax-free, its real value beats a bank FD paying the same rate.

How to use it

  1. Set your yearly investment (up to ₹1.5 lakh).
  2. Enter the current PPF rate.
  3. Choose the duration (15 years or extended).
  4. Set your tax slab to see the taxable FD rate it really matches.
