What it is

An Equated Monthly Instalment is the fixed payment you make on a loan each month. Over a long tenure the interest can rival or exceed the amount you borrowed, so it pays to see the true cost before you sign.

How to use it

  1. Enter the loan amount.
  2. Set the interest rate.
  3. Choose the tenure in years.
  4. See the EMI and how much extra the interest adds over the loan.
