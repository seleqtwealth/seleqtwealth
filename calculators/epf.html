EPF Calculator

Your salary's retirement share.

Simplified: 12% employee + 3.67% employer of basic, ignoring the pension (EPS) split and wage ceiling. Illustrative only. Actual returns vary and are not guaranteed.

What it is

The Employees' Provident Fund builds a retirement corpus from monthly contributions out of your basic salary, matched in part by your employer, and compounded at a government-set rate. The employer's share is effectively free money on top of your own saving.

How to use it

  1. Enter your monthly basic + DA.
  2. Set your expected annual increment.
  3. Enter the EPF interest rate and years of service.
  4. See how your share and your employer's each grow.
