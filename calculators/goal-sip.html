What it is

Work backwards from a goal. Tell it the amount you want, when you want it and the cost of living rise, and it shows the monthly SIP, or the lumpsum today, needed to get there.

How to use it

  1. Set your goal in today's money.
  2. Choose an expected annual return.
  3. Set the years to the goal.
  4. Add inflation so the goal grows to its real future cost.
