Sukanya Samriddhi Calculator

For your daughter's future.

SSY interest is tax-free; rates are set by the government and revised periodically. Illustrative only. Actual returns vary and are not guaranteed.

What it is

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a government scheme for a girl child. You deposit for 15 years and the account matures 21 years after opening, with tax-free interest compounded yearly. The long horizon means inflation matters as much as the rate.

How to use it

  1. Set the amount you would deposit each year.
  2. Enter the current scheme rate.
  3. Add expected inflation to see the real maturity value.
  4. Deposits run for the first 15 years; the account matures at 21.
