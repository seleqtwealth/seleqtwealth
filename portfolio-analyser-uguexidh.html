Only mutual funds? Request a CAMS statement

One request covers every fund serviced by CAMS and KFintech, and it arrives by email in a few minutes.

  1. Open the CAMS page Open the CAMS page (opens in a new tab)
  2. Choose Detailed, from your first investment The form opens on Summary, which this page cannot read. Then set the period to Specific Period, with the From date at your first investment, so the statement carries your full history.
  3. Enter your email, create a password Use the email on your mutual funds. PAN is optional. The password needs 8 to 15 characters, starting with a letter, with a capital, a number, and one of @ # $ * _

Common questions

If you have a demat account, use the one NSDL or CDSL emails you. It is the whole portfolio in one file: shares, bonds, gold bonds, ETFs, and your mutual funds, across every demat account and fund house linked to your PAN. If you only hold mutual funds and have no demat account, request a Detailed statement from CAMS instead.

No. NSDL or CDSL emails the statement to you every month you transact, and every April and October otherwise. To download a past CDSL statement, the CDSL page asks only for your PAN, your 16 digit demat account number, and your date of birth. The CAMS page is a request form, not a sign in.

Look at your demat account number in your broker's app. A 16 digit number means CDSL, and one starting with IN means NSDL. If you have accounts at both, the statement comes from the depository where you opened your first account, and it covers both.

For an NSDL or CDSL statement, the password is the PAN of the first holder, in capitals. For a CAMS statement, it is the password you created on the request form, exactly as typed. Lost the CAMS password? Simply request the statement again with a new one.

The NSDL or CDSL statement is matched on PAN, so check that every account and folio carries the same PAN, in the same holder order. A CAMS statement is matched on email instead, and covers only the folios registered to the email you entered. Holdings that sit outside the securities market, such as fixed deposits, PPF, property, or insurance, are not in any of these statements.
